A centralized land acquisition management platform designed to improve transparency, tracking and decision-making through GIS-based visualization.

- District-level land acquisition tracking.
- GIS-based parcel visualization.
- Database management and structured data organization.
- Dashboards and MIS reporting.

**My contribution:** Primarily database management, alongside collaborative research and presentation development.

`React` `MySQL` `GIS` `React Leaflet`

### 02. JanSevak — Community-Driven Civic Engagement Platform

A civic engagement ecosystem connecting citizens, NGOs and authorities to report, validate and resolve sanitation and sustainability issues.

- Citizen-driven issue reporting.
- AI-assisted report generation and priority prediction.
- Community participation and gamification.
- NGO integration and social impact tracking.

**Tagline:** Report. Validate. Act. Get Rewarded.

`React` `JavaScript` `AI Integration` `Civic Tech`
